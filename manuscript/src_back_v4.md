%% FIG1
Figure 1: Overview of the study and main findings
(A) Samples and time zero. The derivation sample had all ten markers and was used to fit the Subtype and Stage Inference (SuStaIn) model; the independent sample had the seven MRI markers, did not contribute to model fitting, and was staged with the MRI-only model without refitting. Follow-up started at the imaging visit and ended at the first event, death, or Sept 30, 2021; participants with the outcome on or before imaging were excluded for that outcome. The participant flow is shown in appendix figure S1. (B) The ten markers: three cardiac MRI domain scores, four brain MRI domain scores, and three plasma protein modules (the number of Olink proteins in each module is shown). The domain scores are derived from the regional measures listed, which are mapped in figures 2 and 3. The heart is a population-average four-chamber template with the 16 American Heart Association segments outlined, and the brain a translucent template with the JHU white-matter tracts; both are for display only. (C) First ten events of the maximum-likelihood sequences of the two All-10 subtypes, with the posterior probability that the first cardiac event precedes the first brain event (figure 2), and, below, the measured regional signature of each subtype at stage 3 or higher in the derivation sample (mean regional z of the measures indicated; figures 2B and 3A). (D) Primary event rates by stage group in the two samples, hazard ratios per 10 percentage points of stage (model 2), the C-index of SCORE2 with and without stage, and event rates at stage 0 and at stage 3 or higher among independent participants at high or very high SCORE2 risk (figures 4 and 5). The bottom box states the intended interpretation. AoS=aortic stiffness. CT=cortical thinning. FW=white-matter free water. Hip=hippocampal atrophy. LVD=left ventricular myocardial dysfunction. LVR=left ventricular remodelling. M5, M13, and M20=plasma protein modules. PY=person-years. WMH=white-matter hyperintensity.

Figure 2: Model-implied order of cardiac, plasma protein, and brain abnormality
(A) Positional variance diagrams of the two subtypes of the All-10 model in the derivation sample (n=3770). Each row is an event (a marker reaching z=1, 2, or 3), ordered by its maximum-likelihood position; shading, coloured by organ, is the posterior probability that the event occupies each position across Markov chain Monte Carlo samples. Bars above each diagram show the proportion of participants who reached each stage. (B) Measured regional abnormality by subtype and stage group in the derivation sample: mean regional z score at stage 0 (a shared reference) and at stages 1–2 and 3 or higher of each subtype, for wall thickness (painted on a template four-chamber heart, lateral view, with an inset bull's-eye plot of the 16 American Heart Association segments), circumferential (Ecc) and radial (Err) strain, ascending (Asc) and descending (Desc) aortic distensibility, cortical thickness (68 Desikan–Killiany regions; left hemisphere shown), the volumes of the hippocampus and six subcortical nuclei (FIRST), white-matter fractional anisotropy (FA) and free water (axial section at z=+6 mm), and WMH volume. Higher z denotes more abnormal; p is the median posterior probability of the assigned subtype. Measures marked † (wall thickness, subcortical nuclei, and FA) are not inputs to any domain score. The same matrix in the independent sample is shown in appendix figure S4. (C) Position of the first cardiac, protein, and brain event (posterior median and 95% credible interval) and the posterior probability that the first cardiac event precedes the first brain event, for the three derivation models and for the MRI-only model refitted de novo in a random subsample of 6000 independent participants. (D) Ranked posterior mean positions of the {rep_n_shared} shared MRI events in the de novo refit against those in the corresponding All-10 subtype, with Spearman ρ; marker shape indicates the z threshold and colour the organ. WMH=white-matter hyperintensity.
%% FIG3
Figure 3: Regional anatomy of the subtypes and of heart–brain coupling
(A) Stage-matched regional difference between the subtypes (subtype 1 minus subtype 2; β in z units from linear models of each regional z score on subtype, stage, age, and sex among participants at stage 1 or higher), in the derivation sample (All-10 model) and in the independent sample (n=23 974; MRI-only model applied without refitting), mapped as in figure 2B; teal, more abnormal in subtype 1; ochre, more abnormal in subtype 2; faded, q≥0·05 (Benjamini–Hochberg). Replication is summarised by the Spearman correlation of the two difference maps across all measures and across the out-of-model measures (†, not inputs to any domain score). (B) Brain regions associated with a standardised cardiac composite (the mean of the three cardiac domain scores), adjusted for age and sex; faded regions have a false discovery rate q≥0·05. Labelled tracts are among the strongest associations (ACR=anterior corona radiata. SCR=superior corona radiata. ALIC=anterior limb of the internal capsule. EC=external capsule). (C) Associations of segmental (painted on the template heart) and global cardiac and aortic measures with a brain white-matter composite (WMH and free water) and, for global measures, with a grey-matter composite (cortical thinning and hippocampal atrophy); arrows indicate the direction of abnormality. (D) Regional coefficients for the cardiac composite by tissue class in the independent sample, including the out-of-model white-matter FA and mean diffusivity (MD) and subcortical volumes (†), with the median (bootstrap 95% CI, 1000 resamples) from models adjusted for age and sex (model A) and additionally for vascular risk factors (model B); white-matter minus cortex differences are shown below the panel. (E) Observed co-abnormality: odds ratios for an abnormal brain marker given an abnormal cardiac marker at z≥1 and z≥2 (asterisks, 95% CI excluding 1; grey, fewer than five co-abnormal participants), and the proportion of participants with any abnormal brain marker by the number of abnormal cardiac markers. The heart template is the population-average four-chamber mesh of Rodero and colleagues (Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.4593739; CC BY 4.0), cortical maps use the fsaverage5 surface, and white-matter maps the JHU-ICBM atlas; all are for display only and are not UK Biobank participants' anatomy. LV=left ventricular. WMH=white-matter hyperintensity.

Figure 4: Heart–brain stage and incident events after the imaging visit
(A, B) Cumulative incidence (1–Kaplan–Meier) of the primary outcome (stroke or transient ischaemic attack, ischaemic heart disease, heart failure, or atrial fibrillation) by stage group in the derivation sample (All-10 model; A) and in the independent sample (MRI-only model applied without refitting; B), on the same axes. Left, odds ratios for disease recorded before imaging; right, hazard ratios for incident events after imaging; both adjusted for clinical risk factors. Open symbols indicate 95% CIs that include 1; numbers are prevalent cases (left) and incident events (right). (E) Incidence rates (95% CI) by model-implied organ involvement in the derivation sample: stage 0, cardiac involvement only, or cardiac and brain involvement. Numbers above bars are events. In D and E, tinted heart and brain icons indicate the organ or organs that are abnormal.
